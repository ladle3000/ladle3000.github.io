Just Drafts Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Just Drafts is a local-first writing app published by eldal. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Draft content

Drafts are stored on your device or in your browser. Just Drafts does not operate advertising or analytics services and does not send your draft content to the developer.

Your cloud account and storage

When you connect a supported cloud provider, Just Drafts communicates directly with that provider from your device to perform the sync, backup and restore features available in your version. Your note content does not pass through or reside on Just Drafts servers. The developer does not receive your cloud authorization tokens or access to your cloud account through this connection.

Cloud features use your own Google Drive or Dropbox account and consume storage in that account. Notes, attachments, recordings, sync history and retained backups all contribute to usage. Text generally uses little space; photos, PDFs, audio and multiple backups can use considerably more. Your provider's storage allowance, limits, charges and policies apply.

Provider and feature availability varies by client and version. Google Drive sync is being tested; Dropbox sync is not yet available. An available backup connection does not necessarily mean sync is available. Cloud backup is optional.

Account connections

When you connect Google Drive or Dropbox, the app receives authorization to access its own app-storage area. Authorization is handled on your device and used for supported sync, backup and restore operations. Just Drafts does not request access to unrelated files in your cloud account. You can revoke the app's authorization through your provider's account controls.

A direct cloud connection does not by itself mean that content is end-to-end encrypted or inaccessible to the cloud provider. Encryption depends on the feature and settings in use; ordinary sync is not currently end-to-end encrypted.

Encryption and device protection

The paid version offers optional AES-256-GCM encryption. On Android, local encryption uses Android Keystore. Encrypted cloud backups use the recovery password you create, allowing restoration on another device when the correct password is supplied. The developer cannot see or recover that password. Optional app locking uses biometrics or your device screen lock.

Purchases and trial

The app provides a seven-day local trial. Lifetime purchases are processed by Google Play. The developer does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

Retention and deletion

Local drafts and settings remain until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Cloud sync and backup data remain in the connected provider's private app storage until replaced or removed through the provider's controls.

Children

Just Drafts is a general-purpose productivity app and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated as features or legal requirements change. The effective date will be revised for material changes.
