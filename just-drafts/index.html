Just Drafts

A local-first notebook by eldal. Keep writing, checklists, code, attachments and recordings together in flexible note blocks.

Your notes and your cloud storage

Your drafts are saved on your device. When you connect a supported cloud account, the app communicates directly with that provider for the sync, backup and restore features available in your version. Note content does not pass through or reside on Just Drafts servers.

Cloud features use your own storage allowance. Attachments, recordings, retained history and backups can use more space than text. Provider and feature availability varies by client and release.

Privacy and recovery

Read the privacy policy, Private space guide and terms of use before connecting cloud storage or enabling private features.
